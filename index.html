        Android energy intelligence
      
      
    
        Find what drains the battery — and why it happens.
      
    
      
    
        EcoTrace runs 23 energy detectors over a Java or Kotlin project, then
        traces every critical finding backwards through the call graph to the
        architectural decision that caused it. Static analysis is local and
        instant; cross-file causal tracing is handed to IBM Bob 2.0.
      
    
      
    
        
        
      
    
    
    

    
    
      
    
        
        23 local detectors
        
    
          Wakefulness, network, location and architecture rules evaluated in
          process, with no network round-trip.
        
    
      
    
      
    
        
        Causal chains
        
    
          Up to six hops backwards from the anti-pattern to the entry point
          that put it on the path.
        
    
      
    
      
    
        
        Copy-ready fixes
        
    
          A remediation for every rule, written against WorkManager,
          coroutines and modern lifecycle APIs.
        
    
      
    
      
    
        
        Live drain grading
        
    
          ADB dumpsys batterystats snapshots turn the static
          result into a measured mAh/min rate and an A+–F grade.
